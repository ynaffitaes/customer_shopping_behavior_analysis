# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Python, Google BigQuery, SQL, and Power BI. The objective is to uncover purchasing patterns, customer segments, product performance, and key business drivers that can support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis focuses on customer demographics, spending behavior, subscription status, discount usage, shipping preferences, and product performance across various categories.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing patterns
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Evaluate product and category performance
- Assess the impact of subscriptions and discounts
- Explore the relationship between shipping preferences and spending
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights

---

## Key Insights

### Customer Segmentation

| Segment | Customers | Share |
|----------|----------:|-------:|
| Loyal Customers | 3,116 | 79.9% |
| Returning Customers | 701 | 18.0% |
| New Customers | 83 | 2.1% |

The customer base consists primarily of loyal customers, indicating strong customer retention.

### Subscription Impact

- Subscribers represent approximately 27% of customers.
- Subscribers contribute approximately 27% of total revenue.
- Average spending is similar between subscribers and non-subscribers.

### Shipping Preferences

- Customers using Express Shipping spend slightly more per transaction than customers using Standard Shipping.
- Shipping preferences show limited impact on overall spending behavior.

### Product Performance

- Several products consistently achieve higher customer ratings.
- Product popularity varies significantly across categories, creating opportunities for targeted promotions and merchandising strategies.

## Business Recommendations

### Strengthen Customer Retention
Focus retention efforts on loyal and high-value customers through personalized offers and loyalty programs.

### Improve Customer Acquisition
Develop targeted campaigns aimed at increasing the number of new customers.

### Optimize Product Strategy
Promote top-performing products while identifying underperforming categories that may require marketing support.

### Refine Discount Strategies
Reduce excessive reliance on discounts and focus on products where promotions drive significant demand.

### Enhance Segmentation-Based Marketing
Leverage customer segments to deliver more personalized recommendations and improve engagement.
